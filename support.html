Frequently asked questions

  • How do I delete my account? From the app: Profile → Delete Account. Permanently deletes your account and all your data.
  • Does the app work offline? Yes — you can log workouts with no internet connection; they sync automatically once you're back online.
  • Can I change the language or units? Yes, from Profile you can choose Spanish/English and metric/imperial at any time.
